Beyond structure, a few patterns stand out. City representation is uneven with London (9,993 listings) and Rome (9,027) together make up over a third of the dataset, while Amsterdam (2,080) and Berlin (2,484) are comparatively underrepresented, so any "overall" statistic is implicitly London/Rome-weighted unless cities are analyzed separately.
